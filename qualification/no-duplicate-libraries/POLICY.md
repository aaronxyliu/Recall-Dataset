User instruction (2026-11-09): a recovery tool might match only one of several copies and receive a falsely low recovery rate. Exclude the affected library and choose another eligible library.

The exclusion unit is a **repository/library instance**. Inspect the complete previously captured runtime, including global/vendor scripts outside the module-scoring scope. Exclude the library as a whole when the evidence shows repeated copies; retaining only one preferred correspondence is insufficient. A unique instance of that library in a different repository may remain.

Evidence includes: overlapping original own-body tokens mapped into disjoint generated functions (at least two shared tokens and at least 80% overlap of both sets); a full standalone distribution plus imported `src`/`lib` module code; and explicit multiple installed package versions. Exact original-content identities canonicalize source-path aliases. Vendored dependency paths and locked-package source hashes recover source ownership. Separate functions in separate chunks, compiler ancestor/child wrappers and pnpm peer-context variants are not, by themselves, duplicate copies. These checks are bounded to supplied captures and available provenance; they are not a general semantic-equivalence proof.

Duplicates and incomplete evidence are different statuses. Neither duplicated nor unassessed library instances can be scored or introduced as replacements. Replacement candidates need positive audited module provenance and must still satisfy the unchanged source-only rule: production `dependencies`, at least three distinct source functions with at least ten own-body POG features, up to ten libraries per repository. Drop a repository if fewer than three such candidates remain.
